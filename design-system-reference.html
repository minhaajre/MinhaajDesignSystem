The Gap

Every serious decision has a when.

Strategic consultancies map what to do and how to structure it. Risk intelligence firms map the external environment. Neither addresses the timing dimension of consequential decisions.

Voice & Conventions

  • Sentence case for body copy; ALL CAPS + tracking for labels/eyebrows.
  • Em dashes for emphasis; Oxford comma required.
  • No emoji in UI or copy.
  • Single gold accent per surface — emphasis via weight, spacing, tone.
  • No aggressive gradients; flat warm backgrounds only.
  • prefers-reduced-motion respected; focus-visible outline 2px solid var(--gold).
