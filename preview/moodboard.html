Global Rules

▪ No emoji in UI
▪ No aggressive gradients
▪ Sentence case for body
▪ ALL CAPS + spaced tracking for labels
▪ Oxford comma required
▪ prefers-reduced-motion respected
▪ Single accent color per brand (copper or gold)
