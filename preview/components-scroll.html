CCIAF — Scroll to Top + Side Navigation
Scroll this page — dots track the active section · hover a dot for its label · the top button appears after 500px

Introduction

Fixed rail, right edge, vertically centered. One 8px dot per section, gold when active, scale 1.35.

The Framework

Clicking a dot smooth-scrolls to its section. The active dot is the last one whose top has passed 38% of the viewport height.

Traditions

Hover reveals a surface-backed tooltip label at 13px EB Garamond with a 1px border — the only text in the rail.

Engagement

Both fixtures sit at z-index 400 — below the fixed nav (500), above all content.

Scroll to top button: 42×42px, radius 50%, appears at scrollY > 500
